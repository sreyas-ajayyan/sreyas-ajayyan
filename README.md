## About

<div align="justify">

Hey, I'm Sreyas — I build things that live at the intersection of hardware and software. Give me a microcontroller, a few sensors, and a problem worth solving, and I'll turn it into a working system.

I like owning the whole pipeline: designing the mechanical parts, wiring up the electronics, writing the firmware, and getting it talking to an app or the cloud. There's something satisfying about watching a project go from a CAD sketch to something that actually moves, senses, and responds in the real world.

Lately I've been pushing past single embedded systems toward things that are **connected, autonomous, and a little bit smarter** — robots that coordinate, sense their environment, and make decisions on their own.

## How I Work

<div align="justify">
I don't just jump into code. Here's roughly how a project goes for me:
</div>

1. **Sketch the mechanics** — CAD it in Fusion 360, print or fabricate what's needed.
2. **Pick the electronics** — right controller, right sensors, right power budget.
3. **Write the firmware** — the part where the thing actually comes alive.
4. **Add connectivity** — Wi-Fi, LoRa, MQTT, whatever the range and power needs call for.
5. **Build the app layer** — usually Flutter, sometimes Blynk or Firebase, so someone can actually use the thing.
6. **Break it, fix it, repeat** — testing is where the real learning happens.
